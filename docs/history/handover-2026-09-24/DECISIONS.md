## Superseded / Reversed Decisions

[CONFIRMED] The earlier three-project backend wording (`Api → App → Contracts`) is superseded by ADR-002's four-project topology including `MiniErp.Infrastructure`.

[CONFIRMED] Within ADR-007, the original inbox uniqueness marker as the single non-reversible protected-effect boundary is superseded by the MESP-92 `DurableWorkEffectKey`/guard model.

[CONFIRMED] Within ADR-008, the earlier assumption that `internal` durable-work members were inaccessible to `MiniErp.Api` was corrected when the `InternalsVisibleTo("MiniErp.Api")` grant was found; that friend grant was removed, leaving `InternalsVisibleTo` only for `MiniErp.ArchitectureTests`.

[CONFIRMED] The temporary foundation flow `Login → Choose workspace/Tenant → ERP` is superseded by ADR-019's Tenant-host/Overview-first model for normal Tenant users.
